Hi, I’m Charlie, a digital delivery professional based in the UK.

For the past 17 years I’ve worked in digital agencies, beginning in project management and more recently in a Head of Delivery role. Much of that time has been spent close to the work itself, helping teams and clients stay aligned and moving forward.

A big part of my career has focused on introducing and embedding agile delivery in agencies that wanted to scale without adding unnecessary layers of management or process. I’m at my best in environments where teams are trusted to lead their work, with just enough structure to create clarity, momentum, and accountability.

Originally from the Highlands of Scotland, I now live in Oxfordshire with my wife and two children. Outside work, I enjoy reading, cooking, photography, and sports, especially swimming, squash, and running.
